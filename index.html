THE DHARAVI WORKSHOP HERITAGE

The Difference Real Full-Grain Makes

Commercial fashion brands use thin bonded leather scraps coated in plastic polyurethane. Here is how our Dharavi bench works:

01

Intact Epidermal Grain

We preserve the tight, unbuffed grain layer of the hide. This natural grain provides maximum tensile strength, water repellency, and the ability to absorb natural conditioning oils.

02

High-Stress Brass Riveting

Handle anchor points and shoulder strap loops are double-reinforced with solid copper/brass hand-hammered rivets that never strip or pull free under airport travel loads.

03

Heavy Duty #10 YKK Brass Zips

No cheap nylon coil zips that derail after six months. We install chunky, hand-waxed antique brass teeth that glide smoother the more you use them.

04

Bespoke Sizing & Alteration

Every jacket can be custom tailored to your exact chest, sleeve, and shoulder measurements at no additional charge right at our Dharavi cutting table.

PERSONAL TOUCH

Hot-Foil Monogramming & Custom Tailoring
