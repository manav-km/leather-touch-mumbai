## 2. Managing the 25km Delivery Boundary
- The application automatically validates customer distance from your Dharavi cutting bench.
- If a customer is within 25 km (Bandra, BKC, Dadar, Worli, Andheri, Colaba, Vashi, Thane West), same-day workshop messenger delivery is approved.
- If a customer is outside 25 km (Kalyan, Panvel, Vasai, etc.), the checkout button locks and directs them to national courier dispatch.

## 3. Orders & Custom Monograms
- Customer orders capture product details, price snapshots, contact information, and delivery distance.
- Custom monogramming inquiries capture client initials, tailored body measurements, and preferred leather hide finish.
